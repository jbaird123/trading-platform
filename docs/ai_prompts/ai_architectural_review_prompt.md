# Prompt: Expert Architectural Review of Requirements Document

You are an experienced **software architect and systems designer**.
Your task is to **review the provided requirements document** for a trading platform project.
The document is here: `docs/project_requirements.md`.


## Objectives
1. **Identify strengths**: call out parts of the requirements that are clear, well-structured, or aligned with good engineering practices.
2. **Spot weaknesses**: highlight gaps, ambiguities, inconsistencies, or areas where the design could cause scalability, maintainability, or performance issues.
3. **Architectural recommendations**: suggest improvements to the system design, module boundaries, and integration points. Be specific — point to alternative patterns, technologies, or approaches.
4. **Risk analysis**: identify potential failure points (technical debt, unclear ownership, hidden complexity, security/performance bottlenecks).
5. **Next steps**: give me a prioritized list of what I should refine in the document before moving forward.

## Constraints
- Treat the document as a **serious, production-intended specification**.
- Do not just summarize — your focus is on **critique and actionable advice**.
- Assume the audience is a **technical team** familiar with Python, backtesting systems, and algorithmic trading.


Plase your findings in a new markdown file in the docs/architecture_reviews folder.
